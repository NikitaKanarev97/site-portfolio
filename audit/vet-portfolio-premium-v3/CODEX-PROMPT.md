Saving a medical record != publishing owner summary. Owners see reviewed and explicitly published snapshot only. Failed/blocked publication leaves previous published document visible, empty plan blocks; no history browser claim. Private notes and diagnosis are not public to owner, no clinical notes for reception. Missing dose requires calling clinic, never a guessed treatment amount. No email/push delivery or automatic notification. Browser-local prototype is NOT real server-level RBAC. Synthetic patient/data and illustrative treatment; one actual veterinary-practitioner input ≠ validated multicenter research. Do not fabricate user studies, 30-sec savings, actual clinic adoption, revenue, trials, certification or measurable clinical outcome. Document any uncertainties rather than fill them.

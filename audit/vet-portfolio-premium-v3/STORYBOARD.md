**Target:** 45-second recruiter skim; source-backed deep reading for a Design Lead. 8–12 legible primary images is a guideline, not permission for arbitrary cuts.

**Pacing:** use distinct widths and densities, not same 2-column + same bordered screenshot repeated 10 times. Keep Vet's sage and restrained teal; preserve shared portfolio navigation/fonts/spacing. No fake screens/phone shells, left accent stripes, mismatched borders, clipped images, red CTA text, dead whitespace or unreadable diagrams.

**Verification:** EN/RU 320/360/390/768/1024/1280/1440; genuine matched BEFORE/AFTER same capture conditions; desktop/mobile first viewport and full page, major section crops, zoom/focus, source screenshot hashes, actual side-by-side R/B/AFTER boards. Implementation cannot grant itself acceptance.

## Composition and pacing

Ten beats including the opening; 11 visible primary specimens on desktop, 10 on phone (the desktop owner view is omitted). Alternating wide queue, compact connected model, focused trace, native calculation split, asymmetric invoice, state pair, responsive owner comparison, process and three-part closure. Existing Onest and semantic DS roles; sage belongs to Vet. No generated images, decorative UI shells, new global palette, perspective, accent stripe or copied Agent/Learn motif.

The storyboard was adjusted in three deliberate ways: critical states moved next to the decisions they protect; the tall discharge document became a full-source inspection target beside its publication-state family; process/system evidence is distributed across real state specimens and a concise process chapter. White owner composition replaces the initially tried sage field after pixel inspection exposed square white crop boundaries. These choices improve relevance and avoid repeating the hero.

## Independent review focus

Inspect original images at native resolution in the gallery, especially: first-fold authorship versus product scale; model clarity on 320/390 and 1024; clinical-review readability at 1280; publication ready/blocked phone headings and full-document inspection; owner desktop/phone scale; whether the process chapter adequately demonstrates authorship without a decorative component board. Passing browser checks does not settle these visual decisions.

## 2. Narrative — OWN story, not the same template as B2B/Vet
**Core thesis:** "Trust is not a badge. It's a sequence of confirmed actions." The user's question is: who can handle my dog, what do I know while they are away, and what genuinely proves the dog was returned?
Pawly is a consumer mobile dog-care marketplace **concept** with owner/walker roles and an interactive EN/RU demo. No participants, booked real walks, revenue, production verification, live payment, insurance, live GPS, or validated outcomes are claimed. All identities/records/photos/prices and review/safety states shown are demo data.
Build a coherent **single-walk journey across two people**. The verified product supplies meaningful material:
A. **Before booking:** coverage and the pet's needs → Walker List / Profile → Booking Review. Verification is distinct from handling suitability; changing the slot cannot waive risk or invisibly relax requirements. Show a real constraint or alternative state.
B. **During / handoff:** Owner Active Service versus Walker Active Order. Same order, different information priorities. Expected return is NOT proof; show pickup receipt, next action and honest sample map. Home/lockbox access is gated by product demo rules, never make a real security guarantee.
C. **Exception:** Replacement Offer when candidate declines/expires and/or failed photo sending with locally preserved draft. Pick one impactful decision with direct source evidence. No silent reassignment; no invented support/escalation channel.
D. **Climax:** Walker Handover Photo selected locally → send or failed/retry → confirmed return → Owner Order Details ("Baikal is home", pickup 14:05, return 14:52), with real photo. An unsent draft cannot complete a walk.
E. **Cross-role system/trade-off:** compact PhotoProof / TimelineRow / other real components. Optional earnings consequence (owner 950 ₽, platform fee 171 ₽, walker 779 ₽) as a truthful demo calculation, not payment processed.
F. **Outcome:** what was built and exercised, which decisions incurred friction, and important limitations / future real-user questions. Use only sourced counts. Do not fabricate results, testimonials, historical sketches, research participants or numeric improvements.
Target 5–6 strong editorial/visual chapters rather than an exhaustive inventory of app routes. Short high-signal copy, varied section rhythm and image scale. EN copy should sound natural for an international senior product-design portfolio; RU should be independently edited, not mechanical translation.

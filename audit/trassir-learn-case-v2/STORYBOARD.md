## Editorial thesis

EN: **One knowledge system. Two ways to learn.**
RU: **Одна база знаний. Два пути к результату.**

One specialist arrives with a broken camera and needs a useful answer now. Another needs to become ready for a commissioning assignment and wants a guided programme. Both should be able to rely on the same accurate materials without confusing reading with qualification.

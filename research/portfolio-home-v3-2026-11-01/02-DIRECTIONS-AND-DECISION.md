# 02 · Three compositional directions and preferred answer

All concepts keep the current black/warm-white/vermilion signature and five-case order: Agent Ops → Partner Portal → TRASSIR Learn → Vet Clinic → Pawly. The Pawly cover, content, crop, component and navigation are strictly frozen. The six SVG studies in concepts/ are annotated **wireframes**, not real screenshots or approved visuals.

## Option A — Editorial Bridge ★ PREFERRED

**Intent:** The typographic viewpoint flows directly into one substantial, readable product scene. Reduce the long silence between 'Complex systems. Clear decisions.' and the first actual interface. The first project image belongs to the actual first case card, not a duplicate promotional mockup.

**1440×900:** Keep restrained navigation. Use short name+role line and current dramatic 2-line display heading. Below it a tighter two-column support area: 1–2 clear lines of expertise and CV/email/work CTA at left, small author portrait at right (the large photo is no longer an equal-weight hero). Then an editorial transition labeled 'Selected work / 01—05' with first Agent Ops title and human-checkpoint thesis. The upper meaningful 180–240px of the genuine approval screen should be visible in the 900px viewport. Remove/relocate the competing five-item hero index; all five cases remain in the list and available in navigation.

**390×844:** Name/role; two-line heading; short specificity statement and clear View work; compact portrait not a bulky 5-row portrait/index complex; clear first-work label and a piece of the real mobile Agent UI at, or immediately following, the 844px fold. Native mobile UI only. RU text should remain readable without 11px type or broken words.

**Benefits:** Preserves recognizable brand, fastest controlled improvement to first-impression proof, allows calm editorial rhythm, lower regression risk. Grounded in Garri heading, HA artifact entrance, MyTeam readable case metadata.

**Risks:** Simply cutting white space can make the site cramped; the transition needs its own content hierarchy. Test viewport, not guesses. No extra giant number badges or redundant Agent screenshots.

**Decision:** Implement A first. Claude/Codex must not silently pick another solution.

## Option B — Split Decision Stage (backup)

**Intent:** Product proof is present alongside the headline rather than after it.

**1440:** ~45/55 split; identity/headline and short message left, right a native high-quality Agent approval consequence excerpt with one caption about the human checkpoint. Small byline/portrait. Need a DIFFERENT visual scene for the subsequent Agent card or avoid repeat.

**390:** Text, then a true native mobile consequence view, then actions; the image visible before a long page scroll.

**Upside:** Strongest immediate artifact proof. **Downside:** Reduces scale and signature of our existing two-line title; risks making the home an Agent-only landing page and duplicating first case. Only evaluate after an A screenshot fails the fold criterion.

## Option C — Asymmetric 2×2 proof index (exploration, not approved)

**Intent:** Four recognizable product decisions in curated editorial tiles near the top. On desktop: Agent, B2B, Learn, Vet in asymmetrical blocks; on mobile: four full-width sequential blocks, not a 2-column micro-mosaic. Pawly remains an untouched fifth project below.

**Potential:** Display product breadth immediately. **Downside:** Native B2B UI becomes microscopic, four simultaneous stories weaken the hero argument, new art direction diverges too much from the prior accepted system. Do NOT implement unless an independent art director changes the brief.

## Visual acceptance for A

- At 1440×900, reader sees name and role, a memorable assertion, a concrete type of design work, first case entrance, and at least a visibly recognizable portion of authentic UI. No second decorative hero screenshot.
- At 390×844 or first immediate natural swipe, the real mobile product is recognizable and the five-item list no longer eats most of the first viewport.
- Both EN/RU are intentionally composed, work and contact links remain accessible, current Pawly tile stays intact.
- Art direction is compared image-to-image against saved Garri/HA/Dounar benchmark and original Home; no 'premium' claim from successful compilation alone.

## Source discipline
- The local thumbnails are **historical visual evidence**, not newly acquired fresh production screenshots.
- Link each new claimed inspiration to a visible screenshot and a specific UI decision; a list of links alone is insufficient.
- Never put competitors' screenshots in public-facing assets.
- Maintain EN/RU, desktop/mobile, real captures and source attribution.

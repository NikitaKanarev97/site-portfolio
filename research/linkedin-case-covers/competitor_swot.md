# SWOT — case-cover references

| Reference | Strength | Weakness | Opportunity for us | Threat to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| InsightFi | Immediate benefit headline | UI proof is tiny; copy is dense | Keep one short problem/result line | Turning a thumbnail into a presentation slide |
| B2B tender | Strong depth and category recognition | Distorted UI and dated neon styling | Use layering without perspective | Looking like generic SaaS concept art |
| AI automation builder | Single memorable hero artifact | Hardware mockup hides authorship and process | Make one real workflow the hero | Advertising polish replacing case evidence |
| Mediso | Domain understood instantly | Stock people dominate and lower specificity | Add domain through material/color cues | Invented people implying real research subjects |
| Docuverse | Confident editorial hierarchy | No interface proof | Pair concise context with a real screen | Beautiful typography that says nothing about craft |
| Amber Engine | Communicates system breadth | Too many small layers and device staging | Use a controlled second layer for system context | Miniature unreadable components |
| Taletree | Clear central focal point | Interchangeable abstract stage | Use a case-specific generated environment | Generic 3D background shared by any app |
| Colory | Excellent name recognition at thumbnail size | Title blocks the artifact; screen is decorative | Preserve oversized naming but keep UI unobstructed | Treating case cover as a fashion poster |

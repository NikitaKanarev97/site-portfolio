The program allows an estimated 20 minutes to complete the assessment. Each attempt has a 30-minute limit. In the proposed model, another attempt becomes available after 24 hours and learning progress is retained.

Assessment and official certificate issuance are not available in this landing demo. Eligibility, certificate validity and recognition must be defined by the partner program.

Users and roles · 10 MIN READ

Operator access permissions

Describe the actions each employee needs to perform. Separate day-to-day video operations from changes to system settings.

Learning sample · TRASSIRReviewed 7 September 2026
01

List the roles

For example, an operator views assigned cameras, a shift supervisor accesses recordings and exports clips, and an administrator changes the configuration. This is an example: the roles should reflect the needs of the site.

02

Assign the required permissions

Create users and, where useful, groups. Check access to channels and operations. Do not grant administrator permissions just to let someone view video.

03

Test each role

Sign in as a test user and check live video, recordings, export and settings in turn. Make sure permitted actions are available and unnecessary access is restricted.

04

Document the change process

Give the person responsible a list of roles and the process for granting, changing and revoking access. Use individual accounts so that actions can be traced to a specific user.

EXPECTED OUTCOME

Each employee has access to the channels and actions they need, and the settings have been tested under the relevant role.

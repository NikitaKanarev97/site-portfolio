Recording and storage · 12 MIN READ

How to plan video retention

Video retention is the period for which recordings are kept. Start with the site requirements, then check that storage capacity is sufficient and actual recordings meet those requirements.

Learning sample · TRASSIRReviewed 7 September 2026
01

Define the retention period

Confirm the required number of days, channel list and recording mode: continuous or event-based. Requirements may vary between cameras.

02

Estimate storage capacity

For continuous recording, the approximate capacity in decimal gigabytes is: total bitrate in Mbps × 10.8 × number of days. For example, 8 cameras at 2 Mbps each need around 1,210 GB for 7 days. This estimate excludes spare capacity, overhead and bitrate variation.

03

Check recording settings

Check the selected streams, schedule and assigned storage. Check drive health. The capacity available for recordings may be lower than the nominal drive capacity.

04

Check actual retention

Once recordings have accumulated, open the recorded video for each channel. Check the earliest available recording and look for unexpected gaps. Compare the result with the site requirements.

EXPECTED OUTCOME

The retention period is calculated, recording settings are agreed and actual retention is checked for every channel.

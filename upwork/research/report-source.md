Date: 2026-09-04  
Scope: UI/UX and Product Design portfolio covers on Upwork, with a separate comparison against Project Catalog service images.  
Purpose: evidence base for redesigning the eight portfolio covers in this repository.

## Research question

Which visual properties recur in the portfolio covers of established Upwork product designers, and which of those properties should be transferred to Kanarev's eight covers without turning them into generic marketplace templates?

## Synthesis

1. Portfolio and Project Catalog solve different jobs. Portfolio proves work quality; Catalog sells a packaged service. The latter tolerates larger claims, arrows, before/after language and more promotional typography.
2. The strongest portfolio-grid behavior is not “make every card look the same”; it is “let every project own its color and strongest screen while keeping presentation rules consistent.”
3. A large screen is more legible and more credible than three equally small screens. Multi-screen compositions are justified only by the story: responsive system, mobile flow or before/after.
4. Titles should mostly remain outside the image because Upwork already renders them below the card. Repeating the title consumes valuable thumbnail area and can produce inconsistent crops.
5. Red annotation should be used only when the case itself is an audit or a before/after explanation. Adding it as decoration to an ordinary portfolio cover has no evidence in the sampled portfolio grid.
6. Flat, orthographic screenshots outperform laptop mockups for complex SaaS work because interface density remains visible.

## Design rules derived for production

- Output: 2000×1600 PNG for compatibility with the existing repository, with all meaningful content inside a centered 2000×1500 4:3 safe area.
- One dominant real screenshot per cover; one supporting view only when the story genuinely needs it.
- No AI-generated replacement UI, device mockups, universal title bars, stock photography, logos added outside the work, or marketing claims.
- Project-specific background color sampled from the product's own palette.
- Common family traits: flat screen, restrained radius, soft shadow and generous margin.
- No decorative arrows, circles, brackets, handwritten strokes or geometric blobs without a project-specific information role.
- Verify at roughly 360×288, approximating a three-column portfolio card.

## Limitations

- Upwork's direct profile search intermittently triggers anti-bot protection; indexed pages and service/directory pages were used when direct portfolio pages were not available.
- Public badges, rates, job counts and seller copy can change after the research date.
- Earnings statements such as “$100K+” and engagement counts are seller-authored unless Upwork displays them independently; they are treated as credibility signals, not audited financial data.
- The 24-cover count is a purposive sample supplied by the client, not a statistically representative sample of the whole marketplace.

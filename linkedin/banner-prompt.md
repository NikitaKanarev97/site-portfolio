# LinkedIn banner prompt

## Output

- Canvas: 1584 × 396 px, PNG, under 8 MB.
- Purpose: LinkedIn profile background for an international Product Designer job search.

## Direction

Use one real B2B product artifact as evidence, not decorative technology imagery. Place a faithful, flat crop of `public/media/case-dssl/cover/resolution-center.webp` on the left and keep the center-right clear for positioning copy. Use a warm paper-white `#F7F7F5` field, soft ink-black `#1B1B18` typography, and one thin oxidized-orange `#C1440E` annotation. Keep the UI pixel-clean and the overall tone composed, direct, mature, and quietly confident.

Text, verbatim:

> Complex B2B workflows,  
> made implementation-ready.

Secondary text, verbatim:

> PRODUCT DESIGN · DESIGN SYSTEMS · UI QA

Use a compact neutral grotesk similar to Manrope for the main line and a tracked monospaced face similar to JetBrains Mono for the secondary line. Keep the lower-left area quiet because the LinkedIn profile photo overlaps it.

Avoid gradients, neon glow, holographic UI, glassmorphism, glossy spheres, floating devices, abstract liquid waves, generic future-tech imagery, fake 3D, stock photos, extra text, logos, and watermarks.

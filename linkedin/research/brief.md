# LinkedIn Profile Repositioning Brief

Updated: 2026-08-27

## Goal

Rebuild Nikita Kanarev's LinkedIn profile from a Webflow-led profile into a recruiter-readable Product Designer profile for Europe, the United States and Canada.

The profile must make the following clear in the first scan:

- Product Designer with 3+ years of experience.
- B2B products, complex workflows and design systems.
- Nearly three years as the sole designer in a 6-10 person cross-functional team.
- End-to-end ownership from research and information architecture through handoff and QA.
- Published proof across Agent Ops Console, B2B Partner Portal and Vet Clinic OS.
- Technical implementation fluency as a differentiator, not a second profession.

## Audience

Primary:

- In-house recruiters and talent partners hiring Product Designers.
- Hiring managers in B2B SaaS, enterprise software, internal tools, AI products and workflow-heavy products.
- Design leads looking for a mid-level designer who can work independently with product and engineering.

Secondary:

- Founders and product leaders at small international teams.
- Referral contacts who need a clear one-sentence explanation of the candidate.

## Target markets

- Europe: strongest operational fit due to UTC+5 overlap and relevant B2B experience.
- Canada: relevant profile, but work authorization remains a practical filter.
- United States: selective focus on sponsorship, relocation, global remote or contractor-friendly roles rather than generic US-only remote openings.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- Primary identity: `Product Designer`.
- Do not use `Senior Product Designer`, `Design Engineer`, `Webflow Developer` or `AI specialist` as the primary identity.
- Webflow appears only as a hard skill and as a secondary line inside freelance experience.
- Do not invent revenue, conversion, adoption, retention, time-saved or percentage outcomes.
- Do not present the published DSSL reconstruction as the exact shipped interface.
- Do not present Agent Ops Console or Vet Clinic OS as shipped products.
- Do not present Pawly as a live marketplace.
- Do not publish the incomplete phone number.

## Success criteria

- A recruiter can identify role, level, domain and differentiator in 5-10 seconds.
- Recruiter search can infer the important skills from headline, About, Experience and Skills.
- Every major claim can be traced to a verified source.
- Experience reads as evidence, not a list of tools or process stages.
- Portfolio proof is reachable from the introduction/contact area and Featured section.
- LinkedIn, CV and portfolio answer the same questions consistently.

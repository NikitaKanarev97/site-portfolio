# Final Competitive Analysis - LinkedIn Product Designer Positioning

Updated: 2026-08-27

## Executive summary

The market already recognizes the positioning Nikita can credibly own: Product Designer for B2B SaaS, complex workflows and design systems. Comparable profiles are often clearer than Nikita's current LinkedIn presentation, but few combine sole-designer commercial ownership, three complementary public cases and technical implementation evidence. The primary opportunity is therefore not to invent a new identity; it is to make existing evidence searchable and immediately legible. The profile must separate search content from proof content: headline/About/Experience/Skills create discovery, while Featured and case media create conversion. Webflow should remain a supporting implementation skill because leading with it routes the profile toward web-production work rather than product roles. The largest credibility risk is mixing shipped DSSL work with the later public reconstruction or implying prototype-only projects reached production. Geography and authorization will remain external constraints, so the profile should target Europe broadly and the US/Canada selectively.

## Condensed SWOT

- **Strengths:** unusually strong evidence architecture; sole-designer ownership; B2B/enterprise complexity; transparent case disclosures; design-to-code fluency.
- **Weaknesses:** no verified public LinkedIn snapshot yet; limited public production metrics; current career gap needs careful wording; authorization constraints.
- **Opportunities:** demand for 3+ year designers in complex B2B workflows; increasing value of design systems, runnable prototypes and AI-assisted workflows; three case links can outperform generic portfolio-only profiles.
- **Threats:** candidates with local work authorization, stronger brand-name employers, verified business metrics or more recommendations; over-dense copy could hide Nikita's advantages.

## Recommended profile architecture

1. Introduction: Product Designer headline, Kazakhstan location, custom portfolio and contact information.
2. About: 2 short paragraphs - role/level/ownership, then specialization/evidence/technical edge - plus a short portfolio call to action.
3. Featured: Agent Ops Console, B2B Partner Portal, Vet Clinic OS, optionally the portfolio home.
4. Experience: DSSL first; Freelance Product Designer second; no Webflow-led role title.
5. Projects: only if MCP shows that it adds information beyond Featured and Experience.
6. Skills: a prioritized 20-30 skill set tied to roles and education; top three must reflect Product Design, Design Systems and Information Architecture or User Experience Design.
7. Education and certifications: exact verified wording; Webflow course only if it exists and helps a target vacancy.
8. Recommendations: request 2-3 evidence-specific recommendations from product/engineering/client collaborators.

## Key differentiators

- `Complex workflows under real constraints`, not generic UX/UI.
- `Sole designer across three products`, not only project-based concepts.
- `Systems that continue into code and QA`, not Figma libraries alone.
- `Evidence and trade-offs without invented impact`, which makes NDA work more credible.
- `Domain translation`, demonstrated across procurement, AI oversight and veterinary operations.

## Risk controls

- Maintain a claim ledger from `cv/fact-sheet.md` during every edit.
- Snapshot the current LinkedIn profile before changing anything.
- Review the public profile while logged out after editing.
- Compare LinkedIn, CV and portfolio line by line for title, dates, shipped status and URLs.
- Do not use `Open to Work`, relocation or sponsorship language inside the professional headline.

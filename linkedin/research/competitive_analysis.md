## First conclusions

1. Nikita's core market language is already validated: `Product Designer`, `B2B`, `complex workflows`, `design systems`, `end-to-end`, `Figma`, `research`, `prototyping`, `handoff` and `QA` recur in profiles and live roles.
2. The closest peers often have clearer headlines but weaker published proof. Nikita can compete by connecting each claim to a specific case, system or shipped responsibility.
3. Webflow should not be in the headline or opening About paragraph. It is useful as implementation evidence in Freelance Experience and Skills.
4. AI is market-relevant when expressed as prototyping, research synthesis and QA. It becomes noise when presented as a separate professional identity.
5. Location and authorization are practical filters. The public profile should be honest, but the headline should spend its limited space on professional relevance rather than relocation mechanics.

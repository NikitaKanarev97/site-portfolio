# LinkedIn Profile UX Patterns

Updated: 2026-08-27

## Market standard

1. **Headline formula:** role + specialization + problem/domain. Strong profiles do not begin with a tool list.
2. **About opening:** level and domain in the first sentence, followed by ownership and differentiation.
3. **Experience:** role scope first, then product/business context, then selected outcomes or concrete deliverables.
4. **Proof:** portfolio link in contact information and selected cases in Featured.
5. **Keyword reinforcement:** important terms appear in headline, About, Experience and standardized Skills rather than in one isolated list.
6. **Social proof:** recommendations or visible work activity reduce perceived risk, but should not be the only evidence.

## Points of divergence

- **Metrics-heavy vs scope-heavy:** some profiles lead with production metrics; others use team/product scale. Nikita must use verified scope because commercial impact is under NDA.
- **Specialist vs generalist:** design-system specialists lead with governance and scale; general Product Designers lead with end-to-end ownership. Nikita should be a product generalist with a systems and implementation edge.
- **AI identity vs AI workflow:** some profiles turn AI into the headline. The stronger fit for Nikita is AI-assisted prototyping and QA inside the Product Designer story.
- **Portfolio-first vs activity-first:** profiles with limited shipped evidence rely on frequent posts. Nikita already has stronger long-form proof and can use activity as distribution rather than substitution.

## Recommendations

1. Use one stable headline architecture: `Product Designer | B2B SaaS & Complex Workflows | Design Systems` with any fourth phrase justified by target roles.
2. Make the first two About lines a self-contained recruiter summary; do not open with design philosophy.
3. Give DSSL the strongest Experience entry and explicitly state sole-designer scope, three products and shipped partner-portal redesign.
4. Rename the freelance identity to Product Designer and place Webflow only in the final supporting bullet.
5. Feature Agent Ops Console first, B2B Partner Portal second and Vet Clinic OS third; add the portfolio home only if a fourth item improves navigation.
6. Repeat a controlled keyword set across sections: Product Design, B2B SaaS, complex workflows, information architecture, design systems, prototyping, user research, usability testing, handoff, UI QA, Figma and Storybook.
7. Ask for recommendations that validate cross-functional collaboration, ownership and decision quality rather than generic praise.
8. Use posts to distribute case evidence: one problem reframe, one system decision and one honest failure/trade-off per case.

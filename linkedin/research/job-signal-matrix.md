# Product Designer Job-Signal Matrix

Updated: 2026-08-27

## Method

This matrix uses 28 live or recently crawled vacancies across Europe, the United States and Canada. Mid-level roles define the target bar. Senior, lead and principal roles are included only to identify durable market language; they do not justify a Senior title for Nikita. Signals were kept only when they map to verified evidence in `cv/fact-sheet.md`.

## Signal tiers

### Tier 1 — must be visible in the first screen and Experience

- Product Designer.
- 3+ years.
- B2B SaaS / complex workflows.
- End-to-end ownership.
- Design systems.
- Cross-functional partnership with Product and Engineering.

### Tier 2 — should appear in About, Experience and Skills

- Information architecture and interaction design.
- User research, usability testing and rapid prototyping.
- Multi-role, permissioned, data-heavy and operational workflows.
- Engineering handoff, implementation review and UI QA.
- Systems thinking, edge cases, states and technical constraints.

### Tier 3 — differentiators, not the identity

- React prototypes, Storybook, design tokens and code literacy.
- AI-agent oversight and trust-aware interaction patterns.
- Regulated / high-stakes domain fluency from veterinary operations.
- Webflow, HTML/CSS and JavaScript as supporting implementation skills.

## Rejected keyword inflation

- `Senior Product Designer`, `Lead`, `Staff` or `Principal`.
- `AI Designer` or `AI specialist` as an identity.
- Product analytics, A/B testing or growth metrics without evidence.
- Formal accessibility/WCAG expertise beyond fundamentals.
- Frontend Engineer or Design Engineer as the primary role.
- Any shipped or business-impact claim for Agent Ops Console, Vet Clinic OS or Pawly.

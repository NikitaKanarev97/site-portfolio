How I work

Reframe before designing

Most briefs describe a solution someone already picked. The useful work starts when we go back to what the actual problem was.

Constraints are the material

A limit that has survived for years is usually load-bearing. I find out what it holds up before asking for it to be lifted.

Evidence before opinion

There was no researcher on the team, so the research was mine. What a screen cannot prove gets recorded as unconfirmed, not counted as a defect.

Name the trade-off

Every decision I ship carries its cost stated out loud — who now does more work, and what would have to change to remove it.

Write down what did not work

The order a project runs in is a design decision like any other. Where I got that order wrong is the part of a write-up that is hard to fake.

AI across the pipeline

Several models, picked per step, from the first interview to QA on a built prototype. It drafts and it finds; it does not decide.
